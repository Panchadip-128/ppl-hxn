A PLAN WITH EVERYONE IN MIND

A little help finding common ground.

We check everyone's confirmed limits first. Local AI then compares the experiences that fit, using the meaning of your confirmed likes.

Protects the person making the biggest compromise, after budgets, dates, capacity and availability have already been checked.

What does the AI actually do?

The balanced option minimizes the largest modeled loss relative to each person's best eligible choice. Average fit uses mean semantic similarity. These scores do not measure happiness or agreement. Private likes stay on this server; peer text and individual scores are not shared. People choose, reserve and approve for themselves.
